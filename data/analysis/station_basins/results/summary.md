# Station-to-basin rule (PR #99) on current GloFAS stations

Exploratory analysis for spike AB#42674. Generated by `initial_rule.py`.

## Rule

Each station gets the largest HydroBASINS basin (levels 4 to 12) that contains it and no other station, with `SUB_AREA` of at most 50,000 km². Proposed addition, shown as a flag only: drop a station whose basin is smaller than 100 km².

- Run 1 (`station_summary.csv`, `maps/`): other stations are counted per country, as PR #99 does.
- Run 2 (`all_countries_run_*` columns, `maps/all-countries-run/`): other stations are counted across all seven countries.

## Inputs

- Stations and admin areas: NRW-seed-data at `3639e28ae398088b7a38ff361e4970f2f0dd275d`.
- Basins: HydroBASINS v1.c standard, Africa (`af`) and Australasia (`au`), levels 4 to 12. The Philippines is in the Australasia file, not in the Asia file.
- Flood map: `hazard/flood/flood-extents/data-png/{ISO3}_flood_extent_rp10.png` from the seed repo, flooded where the depth is above zero. The GeoTIFFs in `sources-tif/` are not committed to the seed repo; the PNGs are the same grid.
